Check 2: Dynamic search-result cards after a category question

On the home page, a guest asked "What hoodies do you have?". The agent returned the matching product ids as structured output, and the website rendered them as product cards on the page.

Found and fixed during this test: on the first run, the reply said "We have 28 hoodies" while showing 27 cards. The honesty guard (Problem 9) checked prices and stock but not item counts, so it was extended. A reply's item count ("27 hoodies", "5 options") must now match the cards it shows or a count a tool returned; otherwise the agent is sent back to correct it. On three re-runs, the guard caught the agent's miscounted first draft each time, and every final reply said 27. A new automated test covers this case.
